# Community & Social Platform

## Overview
The Community engine powers rider social feed, squad club management, weekly leaderboards, and group chat.

## Features
- **Social Feed**: Story reels and ride post cards with elevation stats and like/comment interactions.
- **Squad Clubs**: Create and join riding clubs with member rosters and scheduled group rides.
- **Leaderboard Engine**: Tracks weekly distance, elevation, and average speed rankings across riders.
- **Group Chat**: Real-time squad messaging interface.
