1. Data Collection

At RiderMate, we prioritize the precision of your ride metrics. To provide our core services, we collect telemetry data including GPS location, speed, acceleration, and device sensor data during active tracking sessions.

Key Data Points:

  • Real-time geolocation (when recording)
  • Device sensor telemetry (gyroscope, accelerometer)
  • Account profile information

2. Data Security

Your data is secured using industry-standard encryption both in transit and at rest. We employ strict access controls to ensure your telemetry remains private and secure.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be shared with third-party service providers solely for the purpose of operating the RiderMate platform (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics).

4. Your Controls

You maintain full control over your data. You can delete individual rides, manage your privacy zones, or permanently delete your account at any time through the app settings.
